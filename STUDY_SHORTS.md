# What makes GTA 6 Shorts go viral: study (19 Nov 2026)

**Data:** 180 YouTube Shorts from the #gta6, #gtavi, #gta6shorts, #gta6gameplay, #gta6funny, #gta6tips, #grandtheftauto6 and #gta feeds. Almost all were posted Oct–Nov 2026, before launch.
- Raw data: `out/study/shorts.json`. Re-run: `PYTHONUTF8=1 .venv/Scripts/python make/study_shorts.py`
- Re-run on 26 Nov: this sample is pre-launch, and real-gameplay Shorts will take over the feed.

## 1. The big surprise: the #gta6 feed's biggest hits are NOT gameplay

| Rank | What it is | Example | Views |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Real-actor GTA-style skits (Chinese channels, cosplay as GTA 6's main character Lucia) | GTA-正义审判, GTA6-罪恶都市 | 135M, 83M, 35M |
| 2 | Relatable family/life skits using the GTA 6 hype | "Finally Mom bought us PS5 for GTA 6" (Sick Plays) | 10M |
| 3 | 3D/animated cartoons tagged #gta6 | Aryan Animation, E06 Game | 2–4M |
| 4 | Hashtag hijacking (#gta6 put on unrelated videos) | magic, chiropractor | 4–32M |

**Lesson:** the #gta6 audience is huge, young and casual. **Story, comedy and "that's so me" beat information.** We copy the feeling, not the content.
- Never fake GTA 6 footage with AI, and never hijack the hashtag. Take-Two goes after AI fakes, and hijacking kills trust.

## 3. Length

| Length | Shorts | Median views |
|---|---|---|
| under 10 s | 38 | 27K |
| 10–20 s | 65 | 260K |
| 20–30 s | 42 | 840K |
| 30–45 s | 25 | 4.0M |
| 45–60 s | 10 | 6.4M |

The big skit channels push the long buckets up. Blogs agree that **how much of the video people watch** matters most (about 65–70%+).
- **Our rule:** 20–40 s for story/moment clips, and 6–10 s for one-shot comparisons that loop.
- Hook in the first second, and never end on dead air.

## 4. Titles and captions

- **Short reaction lines win:** 4–9 words, 1–2 emoji (💀 😭 😂 🔥 are the most common in the hits).
- **Patterns that work:**
  - **POV:** "POV: you finally load into Leonida"
  - **When you…:** "When the cops spawn on a bike 💀"
  - **Number shock:** "13 years apart", "$1.97 billion difference"
  - **Question with an argument in it:** "Will GTA V become a ghost town?"
- **Honest titles only.** A clickbait title that doesn't match the video gets swiped, and YouTube then pushes it less.
- **Instagram/Facebook caption:** put the main keyword in the first line ("GTA 6 hidden car…"). Instagram reads caption text, on-screen text and spoken words for search. Then 1–2 lines, then hashtags.

## 5. Hashtags

- The hits use about 7 hashtags. The most common are #gta6, #shorts, #gtavi, #gta, #gta6shorts, #gamingshorts, #gta5, #viral and #vicecity.
- **Our set:**
  - YouTube: 3 fixed (`#gta6 #gtavi #shorts`) + 1–2 topical (`#gta6funny`, `#gta5`, `#vicecity`, `#gta6tips`).
  - Instagram: 3–5 total. Instagram may limit hashtags to 5, and they act as topic labels more than reach boosters.
  - Never #viral or #fyp spam, and never off-topic tags.

## 6. What ATB Plays will make (fits our rules: no trailer clips, no AI fakes, permission-only clips)

1. **ATB POV memes** (cheapest, highest share): ATB reaction clip on top, our own text story below ("POV: you told your mom GTA 6 is educational"). No footage needed.
2. **"13 years apart" comparisons:** your own GTA 5 PS4 footage next to a permitted GTA 6 clip, with a big number. 6–10 s, made to loop.
3. **Era-ending nostalgia:** your GTA 5 footage + ATB + a question that starts an argument ("Is GTA 5 dead now?").
4. **Crazy moment of the day:** from creators who gave permission, or campaign clips. ATB reacts on top, with a hook in the first second.
5. **HOW TO tips** (after launch, from fan questions): text steps, verified facts only.
